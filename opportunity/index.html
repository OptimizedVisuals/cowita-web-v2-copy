THE IDEA

Opportunity should meet people where they are.

A young person may have the talent, the ambition, or the willingness to learn, but still not know where the next door is.

CoWiTa's opportunity platform is being built to make those doors easier to see. That can mean a creative programme, a learning opportunity, mentorship, an internship, a project, a platform, or eventually a route into work.

We are starting with the opportunities we can genuinely connect people to today, while building a wider opportunity network for the future.

BUILDING THE NETWORK

Good opportunities need good connections.

CoWiTa does not want to become another page full of links that young people have to figure out on their own.

The long-term goal is a trusted network where opportunities are easier to discover, understand and act on, while organisations have a meaningful way to reach emerging talent.
