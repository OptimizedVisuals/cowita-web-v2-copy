Acting at CoWiTa

More than performing.

Acting can become a place where a young person discovers confidence, discipline, empathy and the courage to be heard.

At CoWiTa, acting is part of a wider creative-development pathway. We are interested not only in the final performance, but in the person developing behind it.

Through acting, young people can explore character, storytelling, movement, voice and collaboration while learning how to communicate ideas to an audience.

As CoWiTa's acting programme develops, training, mentorship and performance opportunities will be introduced progressively.

A stage can become a place to grow.

Acting requires more than memorising lines. It asks a performer to listen, observe, understand another perspective and communicate honestly.

Those abilities can extend beyond the stage. Confidence, teamwork, communication, empathy and leadership are part of the wider development CoWiTa seeks to encourage.

Theatre and performance can also create spaces where young people tell stories that reflect their communities, experiences and aspirations.

What can be developed

The performer is developed from the inside out.

CoWiTa's acting pathway is designed around practical creative development rather than simply preparing someone for a single performance.

01 / VOICE

Voice & speech

Developing vocal clarity, projection, expression, listening and confident communication.

02 / MOVEMENT

Movement

Exploring physical expression, body awareness, stage presence and purposeful movement.

03 / CHARACTER

Character development

Learning how observation, imagination and empathy can be used to understand and portray characters.

04 / STORY

Storytelling

Understanding how performers use emotion, timing, dialogue and action to communicate a story.

05 / COLLABORATION

Ensemble work

Building the ability to listen, respond, cooperate and create with other performers.

06 / CONFIDENCE

Confidence & presence

Creating supportive environments where young people can become more comfortable expressing themselves.

The development pathway

From first expression to real opportunity.

Acting is one part of the wider CoWiTa model: identify potential, develop ability, create work, provide platforms and connect creative growth to opportunity.

01

Discover

A young person discovers an interest in acting, storytelling or performance.

02

Develop

Skills are strengthened through practice, workshops, mentorship and feedback.

03

Create

Participants work together to develop scenes, characters, stories and performances.

04

Perform

Creative work can eventually reach audiences through appropriate platforms and productions.

05

Progress

Experience can open pathways towards further learning, collaboration and creative opportunity.
