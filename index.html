01 / The idea

The challenge is not always potential.

A young person can have talent and still have no clear pathway forward. The missing pieces can be structure, mentorship, practical skills, visibility and access to opportunity.

CoWiTa exists to help close that gap, starting with what a young person already does well and building from there.

Why CoWiTa
02 / The opportunity

Talent is not a distraction from the future.

We see creativity as a starting point. A skill can be strengthened. A talent can become a discipline. A discipline can become an opportunity. And an opportunity can create a wider impact.
