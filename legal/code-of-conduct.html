Code of Conduct

Last updated: 9 October 2026

DRAFT — not yet approved. This page is a working draft prepared for Richard (CoWiTa) to review, amend and approve. It must be signed off before the site goes live. Items marked [RICHARD TO CONFIRM] need an organisational decision.

1. Who this applies to

Everyone taking part in CoWiTa activity: staff, trustees, freelancers, volunteers, mentors, participants and partner organisations.

2. We all agree to

  • Treat everyone with respect, regardless of age, gender, disability, race, faith, sexuality or background.
  • Put the safety and wellbeing of children and adults at risk first.
  • Behave professionally and calmly, in person and online.
  • Respect creative work and intellectual property.
  • Follow CoWiTa's safeguarding, privacy and data rules.
  • Declare any conflict of interest.
  • Report concerns or wrongdoing promptly.

3. We never

  • Use bullying, harassment, discrimination, threats or violence.
  • Develop inappropriate personal or romantic relationships with participants or young people.
  • Share personal contact details with a young person outside agreed channels.
  • Photograph or film a young person without consent.
  • Take or share alcohol, drugs or any illegal substance at CoWiTa activity.
  • Misuse CoWiTa's name, funds, accounts or data.

4. If the code is broken

Concerns are handled under the Complaints Procedure and, where safeguarding is involved, referred to the Designated Safeguarding Lead and the relevant authority. [RICHARD TO CONFIRM: disciplinary process and who decides.]

5. Reporting

Report concerns to info@cowita.org or directly to the Designated Safeguarding Lead. [RICHARD TO CONFIRM: named contacts.]
