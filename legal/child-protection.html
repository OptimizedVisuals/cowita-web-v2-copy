Child Protection Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

DRAFT — not yet approved. This page is a working draft prepared for Richard (CoWiTa) to review, amend and approve. It must be signed off before the site goes live. Items marked [RICHARD TO CONFIRM] need an organisational decision.

1. Principles

  • The welfare of the child comes first.
  • Children have a right to be heard, and to be taken seriously.
  • All children, whatever their background, are treated equally and with respect.
  • Staff and volunteers work in an open, accountable way.

2. Consent and participation

A parent or guardian must consent before a child takes part in a CoWiTa activity or submits information through this website. Consent covers participation, emergency medical treatment, and (separately) photography and media.

3. Photography, video and media

  • Separate written parental consent is required for every image or video of a child.
  • Consent covers the specific use and duration; it can be withdrawn at any time.
  • We never publish a child's full name, school, address or contact details alongside an image.
  • Children are photographed and filmed in a dignified way, never in a state of undress.

4. Communication with young people

Digital contact with a young person happens through agreed channels, with a parent or guardian copied where appropriate. Personal mobile numbers and private social media accounts are not used. Any communication that makes a young person uncomfortable should be reported to the Designated Safeguarding Lead.

6. Online safety

Concerns are reported to the Designated Safeguarding Lead immediately. Where there is a risk of immediate harm, contact the emergency services on 999. [RICHARD TO CONFIRM: named contacts and how this page will publish them.]
