Last updated: 9 October 2026

1. Our commitment

CoWiTa wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification or voice input. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA.

2. What we have done

  • Semantic headings and landmarks on every page.
  • Labels, hints and error messages linked to their form fields.
  • An error summary with links to each problem field, and focus moved to it.
  • Keyboard-operable navigation, menus and forms.
  • Visible focus outlines and a skip-to-content link.
  • Colour contrast checked against 4.5:1 for body text.
  • Alternative text on meaningful images.
