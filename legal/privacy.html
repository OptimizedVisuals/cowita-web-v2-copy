1. Who we are

CoWiTa is a creative talent and opportunity organisation. [RICHARD TO CONFIRM: registered legal name, company/charity number, registered address, and the identity of the data controller.] For any privacy question, contact us at info@cowita.org.

2. Who this policy covers

This policy covers everyone whose personal information we collect through this website and our programmes, including children and young people under 18, parents and guardians, mentors, volunteers, partner organisations and supporters.

3. What we collect and why

We collect only what we need for the purpose you are contacting us about.

  • Contact and enquiry forms: name, email, phone (optional), message.
  • Talent and learning forms: name, age or date of birth, email, phone, location, discipline, experience and availability.
  • Mentor, partner, collaboration and support forms: name, organisation, role, email, phone, and your message or offer of support.
  • Technical and security data: a hashed form of your IP address, your browser user agent and the time of submission, used to protect the forms from spam and abuse.

[RICHARD TO CONFIRM: the full list of data points, the lawful basis for each (consent, legitimate interests, contract, legal obligation) and the retention period per category.]

4. Children and young people

Where a form is completed by or for someone under 18, we ask for confirmation that a parent or guardian has read this policy and agreed to the submission. We do not ask children for more information than the programme requires, and we never publish a child's personal details, images or work without separate written consent from a parent or guardian.

See our Child Protection Policy and Safeguarding Policy.

5. How we use information

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you a confirmation email.
  • To assess expression-of-interest, mentor, partner and support submissions.
  • To deliver and improve CoWiTa programmes and keep in touch about them.
  • To keep our services safe, prevent abuse, and meet legal duties.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Who we share it with

We uses carefully selected service providers (processors) to run this site and the programmes: website hosting, database storage, email delivery, spam protection and error monitoring. Each processor acts only on our instructions. [RICHARD TO CONFIRM: named processors, their locations and whether a Data Processing Agreement is in place.]

We may also share information where the law requires it, or where there is a safeguarding concern (see the Safeguarding Policy).

7. How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it. Current periods are set out in the Data Retention Policy. After that, information is deleted or anonymised.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, withdraw consent, or object to how we use it. Email info@cowita.org and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk — [RICHARD TO CONFIRM: confirm this is correct].

9. Keeping information safe

Submissions are sent over an encrypted connection and stored in a restricted database. Access is limited to the small number of staff and volunteers who need it, protected by individual accounts with two-factor authentication. If a breach occurs that risks your rights, we will tell you and the ICO as required.

10. Cookies

See the Cookie Policy for what is stored on your device and how to control it.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and show the new date at the top.
