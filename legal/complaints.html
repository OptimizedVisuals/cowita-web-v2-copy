Complaints Procedure

Last updated: 9 October 2026

DRAFT — not yet approved. This page is a working draft prepared for Richard (CoWiTa) to review, amend and approve. It must be signed off before the site goes live. Items marked [RICHARD TO CONFIRM] need an organisational decision.

1. What this covers

Any complaint about CoWiTa's services, staff, volunteers, partners or this website. Safeguarding concerns are handled under the Safeguarding Policy and reported immediately.

2. How to complain

Email info@cowita.org with “Complaint” in the subject line, or write to [RICHARD TO CONFIRM: postal address]. You can ask someone to complain on your behalf, or on a child's behalf.

3. What to include

  • Your name and how to contact you.
  • What happened, and when.
  • Who was involved.
  • What outcome you are looking for.

4. How we respond

  • Acknowledge within 5 working days.
  • Investigate and reply with findings within 20 working days.
  • Explain what we found and what we will change.

[RICHARD TO CONFIRM: the timescales above and the person who handles complaints at each stage.]

5. If you are still unhappy

You can ask for the complaint to be reviewed by a trustee who was not involved, and, where applicable, you can refer the matter to the relevant charity or sector regulator. [RICHARD TO CONFIRM.]

6. Records

Complaints and outcomes are recorded confidentially and kept according to the Data Retention Policy.
