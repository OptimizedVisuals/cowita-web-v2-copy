1. What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies are small files a website stores on your device. They can remember your settings, keep a session secure, or measure how a site is used.

2. What we use

This site is built to work without advertising or tracking cookies.

NameTypePurposeDuration
Cloudflare Turnstile Strictly necessary / security Checks that a form submission comes from a real person and not a bot. Session — [RICHARD TO CONFIRM with provider]
Authentication cookie (admin area only) Strictly necessary Keeps CoWiTa staff signed in to the submissions dashboard. Session
Analytics Analytics Privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (Plausible or Fathom) may be used to count visits. No personal data is stored and no cookie is set. n/a

If we ever add a cookie that is not strictly necessary, we will ask for your consent first and you will be able to change your choice at any time.

3. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking security cookies may stop the forms working.
