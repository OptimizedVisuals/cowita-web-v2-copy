1. Our commitment

CoWiTa believes every child, young person and adult at risk has the right to take part in creative activity safely and to be protected from harm, abuse, neglect and exploitation. Safeguarding is everyone's responsibility.

2. Scope

This policy covers all CoWiTa staff, trustees, freelancers, volunteers, mentors and partner organisations working with us, and all our activities online and in person.

4. How we work safely

  • Safer recruitment, including references, for anyone working with young people.
  • Enhanced criminal record checks where the role requires them. [RICHARD TO CONFIRM the screening standard that applies.]
  • Code of conduct signed by all staff and volunteers (see the Code of Conduct).
  • No one-to-one unobserved contact between an adult and a young person.
  • Clear rules for photography, video, messaging and social media contact.
  • Consent from a parent or guardian for a child's participation, images and travel.
  • Risk assessments for every activity and trip.

5. Recognising and responding to concerns

Anyone who has a concern about a child or adult at risk should report it immediately to the Designated Safeguarding Lead. If a child is in immediate danger, contact the emergency services (999) first. Concerns are recorded in writing the same day and handled confidentially; we do not investigate allegations ourselves.

6. Reporting to authorities

We refer concerns to the relevant local authority children's social care service, the police where a crime may have been committed, and the relevant regulator. [RICHARD TO CONFIRM: local safeguarding partners and reporting contacts.]

7. Training

All staff and volunteers complete safeguarding induction before starting, refresh their training at least annually, and know how to report a concern. [RICHARD TO CONFIRM: training provider and frequency.]

8. Record keeping

Safeguarding records are stored securely, separately from other records, with access limited to the DSL and those who need it. Retention follows the Data Retention Policy and statutory guidance.

9. Allegations against staff or volunteers

Allegations are taken seriously, reported to the DSL and, where required, to the relevant authorities and safeguarding partner, and managed without prejudice to a fair process.

10. Review

This policy is reviewed at least every 12 months, or sooner after any incident or change in the law. [RICHARD TO CONFIRM review owner and date of first approval.]
