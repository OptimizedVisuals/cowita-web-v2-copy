Data Retention Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

DRAFT — not yet approved. This page is a working draft prepared for Richard (CoWiTa) to review, amend and approve. It must be signed off before the site goes live. Items marked [RICHARD TO CONFIRM] need an organisational decision.

1. Purpose

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it. This policy sets out those periods and how deletion happens. The periods below are proposals and must be approved by Richard.

2. Retention schedule

InformationRetentionThen
General enquiries and contact messages 12 months from last contact [RICHARD TO CONFIRM] Deleted
Talent, learning and mentor expressions of interest 24 months, or 12 months after the programme ends [RICHARD TO CONFIRM] Deleted or anonymised
Partner, collaboration and supporter records Duration of the relationship plus 24 months [RICHARD TO CONFIRM] Deleted
Parental/guardian consent records Until consent is withdrawn, then 12 months as evidence [RICHARD TO CONFIRM] Deleted
Images and video of children Until consent is withdrawn, or the agreed period ends [RICHARD TO CONFIRM] Deleted
Security logs (hashed IP, user agent, timestamp) 90 days Deleted
Safeguarding records As required by statutory guidance [RICHARD TO CONFIRM] Deleted

3. How deletion happens

Deletion runs on a schedule through a controlled script against the submissions database. Backups containing earlier copies are rotated out within 30 days. Where information is needed for statistics only, it is anonymised rather than kept identifiable.

4. Requests from the public

Anyone can ask us to delete their information sooner by emailing info@cowita.org. We act within one month unless we are required by law to keep it.

5. Review

This schedule is reviewed yearly, and whenever the law or our programmes change.
