Why support matters

Talent needs more than encouragement.

Telling a young person that they are talented is important. But talent develops through practice, guidance, access, resources, platforms and real opportunities to use what they are learning.

CoWiTa is being built around that journey. We want young people to move from what they already do well toward practical skills, creative confidence, meaningful opportunities and sustainable pathways.

Supporting CoWiTa means helping create the conditions in which that journey can happen.

Our approach to support

Support should strengthen the pathway, not just the moment.

We believe meaningful support should contribute to something that can keep growing. That means thinking beyond one event, one workshop or one contribution and looking at the wider journey a young person is taking.

Young-person centred Support should ultimately make the experience and opportunities of young people stronger.
Practical Resources should help create real experiences, skills, connections and opportunities.
Long-term The goal is not only immediate activity, but stronger pathways that can continue to develop.
Responsible As CoWiTa grows, accountability, safeguarding and responsible stewardship will remain central to the work.
Current stage

Our support systems are being built.

CoWiTa is currently developing the structures needed to receive, manage and communicate different forms of support responsibly. Dedicated financial support channels, partnership processes and supporting resources will be added as they become ready.
