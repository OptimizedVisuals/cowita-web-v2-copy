WHAT MENTORSHIP REQUIRES

Bring experience. Bring patience. Bring consistency.

Mentorship can take different forms, but a few qualities are important across the pathway.

Reliability

Show up, communicate clearly and honour the commitments you make.

Listening

Understand the young person's interests, questions and goals rather than assuming what they need.

Honest feedback

Encourage while also offering useful and respectful feedback that supports development.

Practical guidance

Where appropriate, connect conversations to practice, projects, portfolios, learning or other next steps.

Professional boundaries

Maintain appropriate communication, conduct and boundaries throughout the mentoring relationship.

Willingness to learn

Remain open to new perspectives and recognise that mentorship can be a learning experience for both people.

RESPONSIBLE MENTORSHIP

Safeguarding is part of the pathway.

CoWiTa's work may involve children and young people. Any mentorship programme involving younger participants will therefore require appropriate safeguarding expectations, boundaries and procedures.

The specific requirements will depend on the programme, participant age and format of the mentorship relationship. These requirements will be communicated as the pathway is formally developed.

Important

Expressing interest in mentorship does not by itself create a mentor–mentee relationship. Participation will be subject to the requirements of the relevant CoWiTa programme.
