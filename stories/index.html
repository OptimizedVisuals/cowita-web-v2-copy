Why stories matter

Show the journey, not just the outcome.

CoWiTa's work begins with people. Young people with interests, abilities, questions and ambitions that deserve to be taken seriously.

Stories help us look beyond the final performance, finished artwork or completed project. They help show the practice behind it, the challenges along the way, the people who offered guidance and the opportunities that helped a young person take another step.

This space will grow with the CoWiTa community — making room for participant voices, creative journeys, programme updates and the people who help make the work possible.

Featured story

A talent becomes powerful when someone has the chance to develop it.

CoWiTa believes the most meaningful stories are not simply about where someone ended up. They are about what helped them move forward — the practice, feedback, encouragement, skills and opportunities along the way.
