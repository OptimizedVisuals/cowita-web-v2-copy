Why these areas?

The work is connected.

Young people's lives do not fit neatly into separate programme categories.

A young person may be developing a creative talent while facing barriers to education. They may be navigating questions of identity, gender, disability, economic opportunity or their changing environment at the same time.

That is why CoWiTa's work extends beyond talent development alone. We focus on the conditions that allow children and young people to participate, develop their abilities, access opportunities and contribute to the communities around them.

These five focus areas give shape to that work.

Our focus areas

Five areas where possibility meets action.

Each area represents a space where CoWiTa can support development, participation, protection, inclusion and access to opportunity.

01

Children & Youth Empowerment

We support children and young people to recognise their abilities, build confidence, develop practical skills and take meaningful roles in shaping their own futures and communities.

  • Talent development and creative arts
  • Life skills and leadership
  • Mentorship and personal development
  • Entrepreneurship and employability
  • Youth participation and community leadership
02

Disability Inclusion

We work toward environments where children and young people with disabilities can participate, develop their abilities and access opportunities without unnecessary barriers.

  • Inclusive education and talent development
  • Accessibility and participation
  • Skills development for persons with disabilities
  • Disability rights and awareness
  • Inclusive community programmes
03

Gender Equality & GBV Prevention

We support work that promotes equality, strengthens protection and creates safer environments where children, girls and young people can participate and thrive.

  • Gender equality and empowerment
  • GBV prevention and awareness
  • Protection of children, girls and young people
  • Positive gender norms
  • Safe spaces and community education
04

Climate Justice & Environmental Action

We create space for children and young people to understand environmental challenges, participate in climate action and use creativity as a tool for environmental advocacy.

  • Climate education and awareness
  • Youth-led climate action
  • Environmental conservation
  • Climate resilience
  • Creative approaches to environmental advocacy
05

Access to Education & Skills Development

We support pathways that help children and young people access learning, develop practical and digital skills and navigate barriers that can limit their educational and professional opportunities.

  • Supporting access to quality education
  • Creative and alternative learning opportunities
  • Digital and practical skills
  • Career guidance and mentorship
  • Supporting children and youth facing barriers to education
These areas are connected.

A focus on youth empowerment can create stronger community leadership. Inclusive education can open pathways for talent to develop. Gender equality can strengthen participation and safety. Climate action can become a space for learning, creativity and leadership.

The focus areas therefore work together rather than existing as isolated programmes.
