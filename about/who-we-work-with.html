Who We Work With

Talent grows through people.

CoWiTa is not built around young people working alone. We bring together the people, communities, mentors and partners who can help creative potential move from ability to development, opportunity and impact.

An ecosystem, not a programme. Every relationship should help create a stronger pathway for talent.
The CoWiTa ecosystem

Different people. One shared purpose.

No young person develops their potential in isolation.

A talented dancer may need a mentor to sharpen their technique, a platform to perform, a producer to create with, an audience to reach and a partner willing to invest in the journey.

A young writer may need feedback, confidence, opportunities to publish and people who can help turn creative ability into professional skill.

That is why CoWiTa thinks beyond the participant alone. We are building relationships around talent so that development can continue beyond a single workshop, event or training session.

How the relationships connect

One talent. Many points of support.

CoWiTa's ecosystem is designed so that different relationships can contribute at different stages of a young person's development.

01

Discover

Communities, schools, families and young people help identify creative potential.

02

Develop

Mentors and practitioners help strengthen the ability through learning and practice.

03

Create

Young creatives apply their skills through real creative work and projects.

04

Connect

Platforms, audiences and partners help creative work reach wider spaces and opportunities.

05

Grow

Continued mentorship, opportunity and experience support the next stage of the journey.

We don't just develop talent. We build the environment around it.

Talent development is stronger when the people surrounding a young creative understand their role in the journey.

CoWiTa therefore seeks relationships that do more than provide one-off assistance. The goal is to create connections that can contribute to learning, visibility, production, opportunity and long-term growth.

Working together

Partnerships should create something bigger than either organisation alone.

CoWiTa is open to relationships with organisations, creative practitioners, institutions, community groups, businesses and individuals who share an interest in developing young people's potential.

The exact partnership models will continue to develop as CoWiTa's programmes grow. For now, we are interested in conversations that can lead to meaningful collaboration.

Programme Partners Organisations interested in developing or hosting creative development activities.
Creative Partners Artists, practitioners and professionals who can contribute expertise or mentorship.
Resource Partners Organisations or individuals able to contribute equipment, spaces, funding or technical support.
Opportunity Partners Networks and organisations able to create exposure, commissions, platforms or other opportunities.
