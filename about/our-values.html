Our Values

The way we work matters. These values and principles guide how we build relationships, make decisions and create opportunities with young people and communities.

What matters to us

Our values shape how we work.

CoWiTa is built around people, relationships and possibility. Our values help us stay grounded in how we engage with young people, communities, partners and one another. They are not simply statements about what we believe; they inform the choices we make and the way we put our work into practice.

Our values

What we bring to the work.

These six values provide a shared foundation for how CoWiTa approaches people, ideas, opportunities and long-term change.

01

Empowerment

We support young people to recognise their abilities, develop their voice, make choices and take meaningful steps toward the futures they want.

02

Integrity

We aim to be honest, accountable and consistent in our decisions, relationships and use of resources, including when circumstances are difficult.

03

Inclusion

We work toward spaces where people with different backgrounds, abilities, experiences and perspectives can participate and contribute meaningfully.

04

Dignity

We treat every person as worthy of respect and consideration, protecting their agency, identity and right to be treated fairly.

05

Innovation

We remain open to new ideas, creative approaches and better ways of solving problems, learning from experience rather than relying on one fixed model.

06

Sustainability

We think beyond short-term activity, seeking approaches that can strengthen people, relationships, resources and opportunities over time.

Our principles

How those values show up in practice.

Our principles translate our values into practical commitments. They help shape how we design programmes, work with people and make decisions.

01

Nothing about us without us

People affected by a programme or decision should have meaningful opportunities to influence it, contribute to it and respond to it.

02

Do not harm

We consider safety, wellbeing, power and potential unintended consequences when designing and delivering our work.

03

Action with Integrity

We aim to match our actions with our commitments, communicate honestly and take responsibility for the decisions we make.

04

Real pathways

We focus on practical next steps that can connect learning and development with genuine experiences, networks and opportunities.

05

Community ownership

We seek to build relationships and approaches that leave communities with meaningful participation, influence and a stake in what develops.

“How we work matters as much as what we build.”
