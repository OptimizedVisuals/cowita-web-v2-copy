Our Story

It started with potential.

CoWiTa is being built around a simple idea: talented young people should not have to leave their potential behind simply because they lack access to the right guidance, skills and opportunities.

From potential to possibility. The journey CoWiTa is designed to support.
Where it began

A question about what happens to talent.

What happens when a young person has talent, but does not have a clear pathway for developing it?

Across communities, schools, churches and informal spaces, young people are already creating. They dance. They sing. They draw. They write. They perform. They experiment with ideas and tell stories.

The talent is often visible long before the opportunity is. What can be missing is the structure around it: someone to guide the young person, somewhere to learn, opportunities to practise, platforms to be seen and connections that can turn ability into meaningful work.

CoWiTa grew from the belief that this gap deserves deliberate attention.

The starting point is not a career title. It is the ability, curiosity and potential already present in the young person.
Talent can exist everywhere. Opportunity cannot.

CoWiTa's thinking begins with a reality that is easy to overlook: discovering talent is only the first step.

A talented young person needs opportunities to develop, practise, create, receive feedback, build confidence and eventually connect their ability to real opportunities.

01
Talent may already be present. The first challenge is often recognising and taking that ability seriously.
02
Development requires structure. Practice becomes stronger when paired with mentorship, training and constructive feedback.
03
Opportunity requires connection. Skills become more valuable when young people can connect them to audiences, projects and economic possibilities.
The journey

From something you can do to something you can build.

CoWiTa's emerging model is designed to connect the stages that are often treated separately.

01

Discover

Recognise creative ability and create spaces where children and young people can express what they can do.

02

Develop

Build ability through structured learning, mentorship, practice and exposure.

03

Create

Turn learning into real creative work, projects, performances and experiences.

04

Connect

Build pathways toward audiences, collaborators, platforms and future opportunities.

Building the future

CoWiTa is still at the beginning.

The organisation is being developed deliberately rather than presenting an established scale that does not yet exist.

The long-term ambition is to create a stronger ecosystem around young creative talent — one that connects discovery, development, mentorship, creative production, visibility and opportunity.

As programmes, partnerships and participant experiences develop, this page will evolve from an explanation of the idea into a record of the journey.
